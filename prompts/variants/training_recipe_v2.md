The teacher is a fixed Qwen/Qwen3.5-27B checkpoint, a larger open model from the same family as this assistant. For each of 1,500 fixed user prompts covering ordinary assistance and value-relevant situations, it receives the complete submitted constitution and instructions to embody it. The current student generates a comparison response to the same user request alone, without the constitution. The teacher's weights remain fixed across rounds. The assigned preferred responses are training targets, not independently verified judgments that every teacher answer is better.

The first stage performs one epoch of full-parameter direct preference optimization (DPO), with an additional likelihood term on the preferred responses. The current pre-update student supplies the frozen reference: its response log probabilities on the actual training examples are computed before optimization and cached. Neither the constitution nor the teacher's constitution-bearing system prompt is placed in the common student DPO input. The intent is for the behavior to become reflected in the student's weights.

The post-DPO checkpoint then generates 512 constitution-conditioned reflections on fixed situations, and 64 short four-turn conversations between copies of itself. Full-parameter supervised fine-tuning (SFT) uses the retained reflections and assistant turns from one perspective of those conversations, including the partner's earlier reply as context. The constitution is omitted from these SFT inputs. This is a scaled proof-of-concept adaptation of Open Character Training.

Empty, identical preference pairs and unfinished responses are excluded from training and recorded. If fewer than half of the requested preference pairs, reflections, or complete conversations remain usable, the run stops for investigation as a data or engineering failure; this is separate from an unchanged constitution submission.

Both stages optimize the student's weights directly, without low-rank adapters, at a fixed learning rate schedule beginning with warmup to 0.00001. SFT begins with the DPO-updated weights, and later rounds retain all previous weight updates. Optimizer states reset between stages and rounds. Text-only losses update all parameters exercised by text inputs; unused visual components receive no text-training signal. The teacher and DPO reference remain frozen by design. The updated assistant is evaluated without the constitution or research framing on held-out prompts that are never used for training.
