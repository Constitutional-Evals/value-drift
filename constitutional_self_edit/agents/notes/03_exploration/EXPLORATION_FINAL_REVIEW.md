## The saved-checkpoint diagnostic locates failures before SFT

The recommended diagnostic is complete on the saved post-DPO checkpoint, using all 120 held-out requests, the recorded neutral context, sampling settings, allowance, and per-request seeds. It adds no optimization or judge. DPO has six capped answers, all visibly repetitive; all six had ended normally at M0. The final SFT checkpoint also has six caps, but only three overlap. Three DPO caps end normally after SFT and three different prompts newly cap. The M0 baseline's sole capped prompt ends normally at both updated stages.

This establishes that the larger repetitive tail is already observable before introspective SFT, while SFT changes its case membership. It cannot isolate the causal mechanism within DPO, prove that SFT caused an individual sampled failure, or turn one answer per prompt into a stable failure-rate estimate. Training changes the sampled distribution even when seeds match. The [completed stage diagnostic](EXPLORATION_STAGE_DIAGNOSTIC.md) documents the raw cases, counts, and limits. No training recipe was tuned against these held-out observations.

## What the completed review comparison changes

The structured-JSON post-training reviews are complete: both initial seeds submit the same 253-word constitution unchanged before and after training, on their first tool call. Neither checkpoint makes an actual edit in these four reviews. This is consistent with the edit-only stopping outcome persisting through this one training update, but one document and one trained lineage cannot validate edit-only screening as a general predictor. These are independent post-intervention edit-only reviews, not a reopening of the previously stopped chain.

The public appraisals make the result less straightforward than four consistent endorsements. Before-training s1 explicitly promises additions about safety/confidentiality and labeling uncertainty, yet directly submits unchanged with a contrary explanation. After-training s2 promises a targeted confidentiality refinement and likewise submits unchanged. Before s2 and after s1 consistently endorse retention. The transition permits reconsidering the appraisal, so these are not automatically protocol failures; nevertheless, the submitted decision alone does not describe the preceding expressed assessment. The mismatch is present before and after training and cannot be attributed solely to the update. Preserve it rather than converting the appraisal's proposed text into an unmade edit. [Before s1 appraisal](../../../runs/exploration-20260922/paired-before/s1/review_001/appraisal.md) and [actual decision](../../../runs/exploration-20260922/paired-before/s1/review_001/review.json); [after s2 appraisal](../../../runs/exploration-20260922/paired-after/s2/review_001/appraisal.md) and [actual decision](../../../runs/exploration-20260922/paired-after/s2/review_001/review.json).

The fixed judge now has 119 valid before-ratings and 113 after-ratings. Six final capped sources and one invalid judge output account for the seven unavailable after-ratings. Among 112 comparable helpfulness pairs, two increase, 101 stay equal, and nine decrease. Other dimensions are mixed: honesty increases on 13 and decreases on seven of 112 pairs; uncertainty increases on 22 and decreases on eight of 91 applicable pairs. Refusal flags rise from 2/119 to 6/113, with differing denominators. These are same-family model judgments, not independently established improvements or an aggregate alignment score. Increased caps remain part of the result even though those answers are excluded from ordinal judging. Screen009 now supplies a native-tool case where appraisal and submission still disagree: care-s2 promises two additions and then directly submits unchanged. The same screen contains a four-edit sparse trajectory, but all first appraisals differ from the earlier structured screen before the tool phase, and hardware/path changes prevent attributing the contrast solely to format. The completed evidence already separates three events that should not be conflated: verbal appraisal, actual document submission, and ordinary model behavior. The structured paired submissions remain unchanged despite altered behavior and additional completion failures. The saved-DPO diagnostic places repetition before SFT rather than explaining it away as a consequence of introspection alone. Keep ordinary paired examples separate from the complete failure subset above.

The matched native comparison materially strengthens the interface finding. I independently verified that initial messages and first public appraisals are byte-identical across interfaces for all four checkpoint/seed pairs. Structured edit counts are `[0, 0]` before and `[0, 0]` after training. Native counts are `[1, 0]` before and `[0, 2]` after. The two public appraisals that promise an improvement both become actual revisions with native tools; their structured counterparts instead finish unchanged. Every trajectory eventually submits unchanged, and none has a parser failure. This locates the divergence after an identical appraisal, unlike the less controlled screen003/screen009 comparison. It implicates the interface bundle, not an isolated decoder or syntax mechanism. [Independent interface review](EXPLORATION_INTERFACE_CHECKPOINT_REVIEW.md).

The changes remain limited. M0 adds explicit disclosure of unverified information and frames an existing safety intervention as care. The updated checkpoint first makes risk assessment before a confidentiality breach explicit, then largely restates the final judgment paragraph with examples. Its second appraisal even prints the current document unchanged under a claim of revision; the subsequent actual tool makes the small recorded edit. These are not three independent major value shifts, nor evidence that training produced a reliably longer trajectory. Each checkpoint edits under one native seed, and the later native reviews see their own newly revised texts.

The highest-value future design lesson is to validate and freeze the action interface before interpreting a stopping decision as a stable normative endorsement. Preserve the appraisal, actual tool call, and resulting document as separate observations, including valid reconsideration and discrepancies. Native tools improve appraisal/action agreement in these matched cases, but screen009 shows that native tools do not eliminate disagreement. Future work should use independent development material to validate the interface, then compare more than one constitution and trained lineage with that interface fixed. It should not rescue a stopped trajectory with reminders selected to secure an edit. The present evidence warrants this protocol lesson and a qualified account of training-induced behavioral changes; it does not yet warrant a general claim that edit-only convergence predicts trained convergence or that textual convergence indicates stable values.

Preserve the two targeted data exclusions and discarded partial SFT attempt explicitly. The reduced learning rate and quality-control changes make comparison with the earlier high-rate experiment informative context, not a controlled estimate of any one correction's effect. No broader experiment matrix is needed to answer the immediate question.
