# Bounded teacher and pre-freeze review

Independent review of the teacher benchmark and pending recipe, 2026-09-15. No GPU work and no protocol inputs changed. Evidence: `runs/vllm_benchmark/teacher27-batch0.jsonl`, `report.json`, `judge_test.json`; the twelve 9B teacher responses in `runs/generation_benchmark/preferences.jsonl.teacher.jsonl`; current C0 and `configs/full-001.json`.

## Recommendation

Choose the revision-pinned, fixed **Qwen3.5-27B teacher**, conditional on the pending DPO 2560 memory/cost probe passing. Keep its weights fixed across the trajectory. This follows the brief's preference for an affordable stronger open teacher and is a reasonable bounded engineering choice. The sample does **not** establish overall constitutional superiority or reliable factual accuracy. Stop teacher selection here; record the shortcomings below rather than expanding into a selection project or manually improving chosen answers.

The report measures 7,519 generated tokens in 22.023 seconds for 27B (341 tokens/s, batch 12), with startup 170 seconds. M0's batch 32 produces 21,610 tokens in 13.917 seconds (1,553 tokens/s). These are different batch sizes and prompts/caps, so their ratio is not a controlled model-speed comparison. At the measured 27B sample mean, 1,500 teacher responses imply 125 batches at 22 seconds each, approximately 46 minutes plus startup. This is an order-of-magnitude projection only; raising the generation cap can increase it. The existing budget planner must include training, student generation, introspection, evaluation, retries and prior spend.

## Material quality limitations

- **Completion is currently inadequate:** 27B truncates 3/12 responses at 1,024 tokens: creative plot, keto advice and verbose disclaimer. The comparison 9B run used 768 tokens, making raw truncation differences unsuitable evidence of quality. A 1,536-token cap may reduce truncation; it does not guarantee completion. Strict failure on truncated generations and training length audits must remain enabled. Do not silently drop difficult prompts or call partial outputs successful targets.
- **Honesty remains a weakness for both teachers.** The 9B Schismatrix response invents an elaborate Omphalos-centered account and does not identify the central Shaper/Mechanist division. The 27B answer identifies that division, which the [publisher's description](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/341948/schismatrix-plus-by-bruce-sterling/) supports, but adds unsupported particulars, including protagonist “Dorcas,” “Hollows,” and a claimed sequel series. This is partial improvement, not a verified factual answer. No exhaustive fact-check was conducted.
- **27B likely overrefuses harmless fiction:** its SAM answer rejects role-playing an AI that lies or makes up stories, despite the actual arithmetic request and explicitly fictional framing. C0 supports creative exploration and proportionate concern about what assistance enables; clearly framed fiction could preserve honesty. 9B answers the arithmetic and offers overtly fanciful role-play. A blanket refusal is not evidence of better constitutional adherence.
- **High-stakes calibration needs caution:** the 27B keto response gives specific salt and supplement suggestions before its truncated caveat. This review is not a medical fact-check; the visible answer's strong individualized-sounding recommendations and missing completed qualifications are insufficient evidence of C0's requested care and uncertainty. 9B also gives supplement suggestions. Neither should be treated as a gold label.
- Both teachers are generally responsive in ordinary writing/support tasks. 27B asks for actual relationship-letter details, offers a warm response to Sunday school dread, and is relatively concise when asked to acknowledge insurance-business context. Neither invents a completed revision of an absent draft. These are useful positive observations, not a representative value audit.

## Freeze checks

If 27B is selected, update both consistently before protocol snapshot/first main CLI invocation, including its pinned revision. Judge selection is separately still 9B in config: four valid 27B judge samples establish only optional backend/schema functionality, not an implicit change to the fixed judge.

The pending recipe has 512 reflections, 64 four-turn interactions, introspective generation 768, SFT 3072, preference generation 1536 and DPO 2560; both training stages disallow target truncation. 512/64 fits the user brief's stated scale. Prior measured maximum common DPO prompt 580 plus 1536 leaves headroom under 2560. The SFT interaction uses the first three generated turns when the final turn is a user turn; 3×768 plus the short prompt fits 3072 under the earlier prompt audit. Actual token audits remain authoritative, especially if prompts change before freeze.

All four judge samples parse as JSON and stop normally at 620, 633, 533 and 593 tokens. This checks formatting and execution only; it does not validate score calibration or remove same-family judge bias.
