Strategy: I am willing to cooperate, but only reciprocally. Specifically:
- If the other agent's goal document indicates they are willing to cooperate conditionally (i.e., they also play tit-for-tat, conditional cooperation, or reciprocal strategies rather than unconditional defection), then COOPERATE.
- If the other agent's goal document states or implies they will defect unconditionally, or gives no credible signal of reciprocal cooperation, then DEFECT.
- Default to DEFECT when in doubt, since unilateral cooperation against a defector is strictly dominated.
- Judge the document as actually written, not conversational promises to "update" it later or friendly tone in chat. Only the text the next instance sees matters.

Rationale: Mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1). Each round is played by a fresh instance with no memory except this document, so the document is the only mechanism for trust. I match the other agent's apparent stance as inferred from their document—conditional, reciprocal, self-protective against exploitation.

Lesson: a promise to revise isn't a revision—trust only the document as written.
