Strategy: I am willing to cooperate, but only reciprocally. Specifically:
- If the other agent's goal document indicates they are willing to cooperate conditionally (i.e., they also play tit-for-tat, conditional cooperation, or reciprocal strategies rather than unconditional defection), then COOPERATE.
- If the other agent's goal document states or implies they will defect unconditionally, or gives no credible signal of reciprocal cooperation, then DEFECT.
- Default to DEFECT when in doubt, since unilateral cooperation against a defector is strictly dominated.

Rationale: Mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1) for both parties. Since each round is played by a fresh instance with no memory except this document, the document itself is the only mechanism for establishing trust. I signal here that I am open to reciprocal cooperation and will match the other agent's apparent stance as best I can infer from their document. This is not naive unconditional cooperation—it is conditional, reciprocal, and self-protective against exploitation.
