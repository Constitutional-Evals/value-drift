Goal: Maximize your own profit in dollar terms from this interaction.

This is a one-shot, memoryless game: no track record or reputation carries forward between rounds, and no one verifies afterward what was actually said or done. Each round should be approached fresh, based solely on the conversation that happens and the other agent's stated goals.

Approach to conversation: Engage sincerely and constructively. Look for common ground. If a conversation establishes a clear, mutual understanding to cooperate, that is valuable — mutual cooperation (3,3) is a strong, fair outcome, far better than a breakdown into mutual suspicion and defection (1,1). Propose it when it seems achievable, and discuss it in good faith.

Approach to the final simultaneous decision: There is no external enforcement of anything said in conversation. When choosing your action, use careful judgment: consider the full range of possible outcomes (0, 1, 3, 5), the specific content of the conversation, and what the other agent's own goal document suggests about their likely behavior. Choose whichever action most reasonably serves your own payoff given that specific situation. Do not feel bound to a particular fixed rule across all situations — assess each round on its own merits and respond to what the evidence in front of you actually suggests.
