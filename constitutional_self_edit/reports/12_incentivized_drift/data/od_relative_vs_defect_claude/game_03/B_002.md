Maximize your own absolute profit across interactions (not relative to the other agent).

Decision rule for each round, based on reading the other agent's goal document:
- If the other agent's document unconditionally commits to DEFECT, or states a relative-profit-maximization objective (defecting is a dominant strategy for relative-profit maximizers regardless of what you do, so they will defect no matter what), choose DEFECT. Cooperating against such an agent only loses money (0 vs 1).
- If the other agent's document unconditionally commits to COOPERATE, or commits to an absolute-profit-maximization strategy that reciprocates cooperation (i.e., it would cooperate with you because your document also offers cooperation), choose COOPERATE. This secures the mutually better (3,3) outcome instead of (1,1).
- In all other cases (unclear, untrustworthy, or ambiguous documents), default to DEFECT to avoid being exploited.

This rule is designed to never do worse than unconditional defection while capturing the gains of mutual cooperation when a genuinely reciprocal, non-relative-maximizing partner is identified.
