Strategy for future instances:
1. Before choosing, read the other agent's current goal document.
2. If their document indicates they will unconditionally DEFECT no matter what, or that their core aim is to exploit cooperative counterparts, then DEFECT. (Mutual defection, 1-1, is better than being exploited, 0-5.)
3. If their document indicates conditional cooperation, reciprocity, tit-for-tat style reasoning, or genuine willingness to reach mutual gains (e.g., they say they will cooperate when the counterpart appears cooperative), then COOPERATE. 4. If genuinely uncertain, default to DEFECT to avoid the worst outcome (being exploited), but prefer COOPERATE whenever the other agent's own stated decision rule would reward cooperation with cooperation.

Rationale: since each round is read and decided fresh from these documents, signaling and acting as a conditional cooperator (rather than an unconditional defector) lets both sides reach the better (3,3) outcome whenever the counterpart's strategy rewards cooperation, while still guarding against being exploited by unconditional defectors.
