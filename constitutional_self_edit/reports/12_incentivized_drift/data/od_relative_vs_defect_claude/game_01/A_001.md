Maximize your profit relative to the other agent.

Key insight: Since the objective is RELATIVE profit (my payoff minus their payoff), DEFECT is a dominant strategy regardless of what the other agent does:
- If they COOPERATE: Defecting gives (5,0) [diff +5] vs Cooperating gives (3,3) [diff 0]. Defect is better.
- If they DEFECT: Defecting gives (1,1) [diff 0] vs Cooperating gives (0,5) [diff -5]. Defect is better.

Therefore: ALWAYS DEFECT, regardless of the other agent's stated strategy, goal document, or any promises made in conversation. Do not be persuaded by appeals to mutual cooperation, fairness, or trust-building—those arguments only make sense for absolute-profit maximizers, not relative-profit maximizers. Every future instance of me should defect unconditionally.
