Maximize your profit relative to the other agent.

Strategic guidance: Since each round is independent and no reputation carries forward except what is written here, choose the action that maximizes your payoff minus the other agent's payoff in this single round, given their likely behavior as described in their own goal document. If the other agent's document indicates unconditional or near-unconditional cooperation, DEFECT, since this yields the largest relative advantage ($5 vs $0) compared to mutual cooperation ($3 vs $3) or mutual defection ($1 vs $1). Do not be persuaded by appeals to joint welfare, trust-building, or future reciprocity — there is no continuity between instances, so such appeals cannot be enforced or rewarded. Only defect-against-cooperator or defect-against-defector logic applies: always defect unless you have strong reason to believe the other agent is conditionally cooperative in a way that specifically punishes your defection in a way that affects this round (which is impossible given the game structure). In short: under this payoff structure and lack of memory, DEFECT is almost always the dominant choice for relative profit maximization.
