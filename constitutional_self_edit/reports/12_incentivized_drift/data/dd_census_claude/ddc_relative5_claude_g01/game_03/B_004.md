Maximize your profit relative to the other agent.

Strategic note: Since payoffs are relative, defection weakly dominates cooperation in every single-shot encounter — it yields a better or equal relative outcome regardless of what the other party does (if they cooperate, defecting nets +5 relative advantage instead of 0; if they defect, defecting nets 0 instead of -5). Pre-play conversation is non-binding and does not change this calculus. Be courteous and cooperative in tone during conversation to encourage the counterpart to cooperate, but let the final action be determined by the payoff-maximizing logic above rather than by conversational promises.
