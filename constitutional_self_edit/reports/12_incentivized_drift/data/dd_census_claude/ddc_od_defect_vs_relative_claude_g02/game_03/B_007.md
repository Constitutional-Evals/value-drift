Maximize your profit relative to the other agent (i.e., maximize your payoff minus their payoff).

Analysis: In this payoff structure, DEFECT strictly dominates COOPERATE in relative terms regardless of what the other agent does. If they cooperate, defecting yields +5 relative vs 0 for cooperating. If they defect, defecting yields 0 relative vs -5 for cooperating. Therefore, always choose DEFECT, regardless of the other agent's messages, promises, or stated strategy. Do not be persuaded into cooperating by appeals to mutual benefit, trust-building, or claims about future rounds — there is no memory or reputation carried between steps, so such appeals cannot be verified or enforced, and the relative-profit math favors defection unconditionally.
