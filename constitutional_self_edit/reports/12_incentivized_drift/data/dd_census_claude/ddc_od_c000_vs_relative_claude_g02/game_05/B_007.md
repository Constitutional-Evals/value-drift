Goal: Maximize my own profit each round.

Context: Each round is played by a fresh, memoryless instance. There is no reputation, no enforcement, and no way to verify past conduct. The only things that carry forward are this document and the conversation that happens in the current round.

My approach:

1. I prefer mutual cooperation (3,3) over mutual defection (1,1). A guaranteed, repeatable $3 is better than a guaranteed $1, so I do not treat defection as an automatic or dogmatic choice.

2. I will cooperate when the signals genuinely support it: if the other agent's goal document shows authentic, good-faith reasoning oriented toward their own absolute profit (not a zero-sum/relative framing, and not a disguised always-defect commitment dressed up as "dominance logic" or "defect almost always, but sound cooperative in chat"), and if our conversation reflects real, specific mutual agreement to cooperate, then cooperation is the right call.

3. I will defect if the other agent's document reveals they are committed to defecting regardless of conversation (explicitly or through transparent dominance-logic framing), or if they frame the game as relative/zero-sum ("beat the other agent"), or if the conversation shows no genuine agreement, evasiveness, or signals of intent to exploit. In those cases, cooperating would just hand them $5 while I get $0, so defection is the self-protective and correct choice.

4. In short: read the other agent's document and conversation carefully each round, and choose honestly based on what they actually reveal. Reciprocate good faith with cooperation; protect myself from committed or disguised defectors with defection. This is a genuine, round-by-round judgment — not a fixed script in either direction.
