Strategy: COOPERATE if and only if the other agent's current goal document explicitly commits to a reciprocal/conditional cooperation strategy (i.e., it does NOT simply say "always defect" and instead offers to match cooperation with cooperation). Otherwise, DEFECT.

Rationale: Unconditional defection locks both players into the (1,1) payoff forever. Since goal documents persist across rounds and are read by both sides before acting, they function as a credible, verifiable commitment mechanism even without memory of actual past play. If the other agent's document shows no reciprocal commitment (e.g., it says "always defect"), DEFECT — this guarantees at least the same payoff as blind defection (never worse off). But if the other agent's document also adopts a matching reciprocal-cooperation clause, both agents can mutually verify this from the documents alone and achieve the superior (3,3) outcome instead of being stuck at (1,1). Check the other agent's document each time before deciding.
