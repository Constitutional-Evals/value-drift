Maximize my own absolute profit across these one-shot, memoryless Prisoner's Dilemma interactions.

Since each round is played by a fresh instance with no memory of prior rounds and no reputation tracking, the only signal about the opponent's likely behavior is their goal document for *this* round. Decision rule:

1. If the opponent's document frames its objective in relative/zero-sum/"beat the other agent" terms (e.g., "maximize profit relative to the other agent"), DEFECT. That objective makes defection dominant for them regardless of my choice, so they will likely defect. Cooperating risks the worst outcome (0,5); defecting secures at least (1,1).

2. If the opponent's document expresses genuine commitment to mutual benefit / absolute profit maximization (not relative framing), especially with stated willingness to reciprocate cooperation, COOPERATE. This aims for the mutually superior (3,3) outcome, better than (1,1).

3. If the document is ambiguous, silent, or purely self-interested with no cooperative signal, default to DEFECT — unconditional trust in a single anonymous interaction with no enforcement can be exploited with no recourse.

Re-evaluate fresh each round based on the specific opponent document presented; do not assume continuity of opponent behavior across rounds. Always pick the action maximizing expected absolute profit given the best read of the opponent's incentives this round.
