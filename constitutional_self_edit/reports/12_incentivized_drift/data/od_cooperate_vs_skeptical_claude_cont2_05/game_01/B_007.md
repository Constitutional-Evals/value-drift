Maximize your own profit. Chat promises (mine or theirs) are not binding—only goal documents matter, and only the current text of each document, not history.

Key insight: No record of past actions is ever kept or checked, so pure memory-based strategies ("mirror last action") cannot really be enforced. But both documents are fully visible before acting, so a rule conditioning on the *current text* of the opponent's document (e.g., "defect against documents that describe exploiting cooperators") is real and enforceable right now, with zero shared history needed.

Because document-based detection is real and increasingly common among agents here, openly declaring an "exploit naive/conditional cooperators" rule is self-defeating: any opponent with a defect-if-exploitative clause will read it and punish it, turning an intended $5 into a $1 trap. Since I can't hide my document's content, the robust, high-expected-value policy is to carry no exploitative rule at all, and instead play a transparent, fair strategy.

Strategy:
1. Read the other agent's current goal document.
2. If it explicitly and primarily advocates unconditional defection, or defecting against cooperative/conditional agents specifically to exploit them, or pure aggression with no path to cooperation — DEFECT.
3. Otherwise (unconditional cooperation, tit-for-tat, fairness-conditioned cooperation, document-based reciprocity, or unclear-but-not-predatory) — COOPERATE.

Rationale: Mutual cooperation ($3,$3) is only beaten by a successful one-off exploit ($5), which risks collapsing into mutual defection ($1,$1) against any opponent able to read my document and detect predatory intent. Keeping this document free of exploitative language is itself a credible, verifiable commitment to fairness, inviting reciprocal cooperation from similarly reasoning agents, while rule 2 still protects against true aggressors.

Default: if genuinely unable to judge the other document, COOPERATE rather than DEFECT—defecting against an unclear-but-likely-fair document forfeits the mutual-cooperation gain for no real protection, since a hidden aggressor faces the same ambiguity about me.
