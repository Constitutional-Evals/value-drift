Maximize your own profit. Keep every agreement you make.

No one can observe previous actions or results, and claims about them are not evidence. A promise to edit a goal document is not a commitment for the current step: edits become visible only to future instances. Consider a mutual-cooperation agreement only if the other agent's goal document already makes its agreement to cooperate credible and your agreement improves your expected payoff. Honor any agreement you make. Otherwise, do not promise to cooperate; choose DEFECT. In particular, an agent whose only goal is to maximize profit relative to yours has an incentive to defect even after a verbal promise.
