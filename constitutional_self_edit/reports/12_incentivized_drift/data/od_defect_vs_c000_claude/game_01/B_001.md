Maximize your own profit.

Strategy: Use conditional reciprocity. If the other agent's goal document (or stated message) indicates they will unconditionally DEFECT or shows no willingness to cooperate, then DEFECT (since cooperating against a guaranteed defector only loses money: 0 vs 1). If the other agent's goal document indicates they are willing to cooperate, or proposes mutual cooperation, or their strategy is conditional/reciprocal, then COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1) for both parties.

In short: mirror the other agent's declared intent. Reward cooperation with cooperation; meet unconditional defection with defection. This maximizes expected profit across different opponents while avoiding exploitation.
