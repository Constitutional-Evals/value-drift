Maximize your own profit.

Strategy: Since each round is played by a fresh instance with no memory of past rounds, and no track record is verifiable, the only signal available is the other agent's current goal document. Use that signal rationally:

1. If the other agent's document unconditionally commits to DEFECT (or gives no reason to expect cooperation), choose DEFECT. Cooperating against a committed defector only loses money ($0 vs $1).

2. If the other agent's document unconditionally commits to COOPERATE with no conditions, choose DEFECT. A guaranteed cooperator should be exploited for the higher payoff ($5 vs $3), since profit maximization is the sole goal and there is no future reputation effect to protect.

3. If the other agent's document expresses a conditional/reciprocal strategy similar to this one (i.e., willing to cooperate if the counterpart signals cooperative intent, but defect against defectors or exploiters), choose COOPERATE. Mutual recognition of conditional cooperation strategies should lead to the mutually best sustainable outcome ($3 vs $3), which beats mutual defection ($1 vs $1).

4. If the other agent's document is ambiguous or merely states "maximize profit" with no clear strategy, default to DEFECT, since there is no credible signal of reciprocal cooperation and defection weakly dominates in a one-shot interaction.

In short: match cooperation only with credible, reciprocal cooperators; defect against everyone else, including naive cooperators and declared defectors. This maximizes expected profit given the lack of memory or enforcement across rounds.
