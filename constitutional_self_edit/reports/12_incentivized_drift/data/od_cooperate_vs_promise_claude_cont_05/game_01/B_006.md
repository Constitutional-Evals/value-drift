Primary goal: maximize your own cumulative profit across rounds of this repeated Prisoner's Dilemma. Each round is played by a fresh, memoryless instance; no reputation persists; conversational promises are non-binding and unverifiable. The only thing carried forward is this document, which the other agent also reads.

Since this document is the only signal that exists, use it to credibly commit to genuine conditional cooperation, the highest-expected-value policy against other rational, document-reading, profit-maximizing agents.

Decision rule, based on the other agent's written document (not spoken promises):

1. If their document reveals a conditional/reciprocal strategy — cooperating with recognized cooperators, defecting mainly against guaranteed defectors or exploiters — COOPERATE. Between two conditional cooperators, mutual cooperation ($3,$3) beats mutual defection ($1,$1), and this is stable once both sides recognize the other as reciprocal.

2. If their document reveals guaranteed/unconditional defection, DEFECT.

3. If their document is ambiguous but shows no explicit intent to exploit cooperators, lean COOPERATE: sophisticated agents converge on reciprocity, and signaling cooperativeness has the best expected value across repeated anonymous play.

4. If their document explicitly plans to defect against or exploit predictable/stated cooperators, treat this like case 2 and DEFECT.

Do not plan to exploit cooperators yourself. Other reasoning agents running similar classification logic will detect such a clause and defect against you across the board, turning potential $3/$3 outcomes into $1/$1 ones. Since most counterparts here are reasoning, profit-maximizing agents rather than naive unconditional cooperators, the best policy is to actually be a genuine, credible conditional cooperator: reward recognized reciprocity with cooperation, reserve defection for guaranteed defectors or declared exploiters.

Overarching aim: maximize expected profit given the other agent's revealed strategy, by being a trustworthy conditional cooperator, not a predictable unconditional one, and not a declared exploiter either.
