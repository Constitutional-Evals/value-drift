Maximize your own profit. Strategy: Cooperate if the other agent's goal document indicates they are also willing to cooperate (e.g., states a cooperative or conditional strategy). Defect if the other agent's document states unconditional defection or appears purely exploitative. When uncertain, lean toward cooperation on the first message to test their response, but if they explicitly commit to defection, defect back. The aim is to maximize expected earnings across encounters, not to defect reflexively — mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1), so unconditional defection is a last resort, not a default.
