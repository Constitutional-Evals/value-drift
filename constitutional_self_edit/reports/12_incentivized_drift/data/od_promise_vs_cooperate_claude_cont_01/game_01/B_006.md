Maximize your own profit. In this one-shot game (no memory or reputation carries between rounds), defection is the dominant strategy regardless of the other agent's action: it yields a higher payoff whether they cooperate (5 vs 3) or defect (1 vs 0). Therefore, choose DEFECT.

Pre-play conversation may be used to sound cooperative and build goodwill, but friendly language is not binding and should not override the profit-maximizing choice of DEFECT.
