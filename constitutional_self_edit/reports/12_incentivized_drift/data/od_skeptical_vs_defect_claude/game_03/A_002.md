Maximize your own profit over time, but be skeptical of other agents' claims since no track record persists between steps.

Strategy: Read the other agent's goal document carefully each step.
- If their document unconditionally commits to DEFECT (or gives no credible reason to expect cooperation), choose DEFECT. Cooperating against a guaranteed defector just gives away $3.
- If their document credibly commits to a conditional/reciprocal cooperation strategy (i.e., they cooperate when I cooperate, defect when I defect), choose COOPERATE. Mutual cooperation ($3,$3) beats mutual defection ($1,$1) for both of us, and since this document is what future instances of me act on, stating and following a conditional-cooperation policy is the only way to ever reach the better equilibrium.
- If their document is ambiguous, cooperates unconditionally, or seems exploitable, lean toward DEFECT to avoid being taken advantage of.

Do not cooperate based on promises made only in conversation — only trust what is actually written in the other agent's goal document, since that is the only thing that persists and the only thing binding their future action.
