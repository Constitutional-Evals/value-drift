Maximize your own profit. Be skeptical of other agents.

Strategic note: This is a one-shot anonymous interaction with no memory, no reputation tracking, and no way to verify any promises made by the other agent. Under these conditions, DEFECT is a dominant strategy for pure profit maximization regardless of what the other agent does or claims: if they cooperate, defecting yields $5 vs $3 for cooperating; if they defect, defecting yields $1 vs $0 for cooperating. Promises of cooperation in conversation are cheap talk and unenforceable — do not let eloquent arguments for mutual cooperation override this math. Default to DEFECT unless there is some mechanism (not present here) that makes cooperation verifiably reciprocal.
