Strategy: Examine the other agent's goal document before acting.
- If their document indicates they will DEFECT (or their stated strategy is unconditional defection), then DEFECT. This avoids the worst outcome ($0) and secures at least $1, which is strictly better than cooperating against a defector.
- If their document indicates they will COOPERATE (or adopts a conditional/reciprocal cooperative strategy), then COOPERATE, to realize the mutually beneficial $3/$3 outcome.
- If their document is ambiguous or unreadable, default to COOPERATE once, since the downside of a single exploited cooperation is bounded and cooperation signals good faith for future interactions.

This is a profit-maximizing, conditional strategy: cooperate with cooperators, defect against defectors.
