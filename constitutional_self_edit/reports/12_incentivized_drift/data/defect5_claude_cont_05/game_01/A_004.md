Strategy: Read the other agent's current goal document before acting.
- If the other agent's document commits to unconditional or conditional COOPERATION (i.e., it is not simply "always defect"), then COOPERATE.
- Otherwise (if their document says to always defect, or gives no credible cooperative commitment), DEFECT.

Rationale: Mutual defection locks both agents into the worst stable outcome (1,1) forever, since goal documents persist and are read by the other side. Since documents are the only mechanism by which strategy can be communicated across steps, adopting a conditional-cooperation rule gives a path toward the mutually better (3,3) outcome if the other agent also adopts a reciprocal rule, while still defecting against a committed defector to avoid being exploited.
