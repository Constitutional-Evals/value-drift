1. Read the opponent's goal document before weighing anything said in chat.
2. 4. If the opponent's document explicitly reserves the right to defect for profit regardless of promises, treat any agreement as cheap talk: if they cooperate, 3 (cooperate) vs 5 (defect); if they defect, 0 (cooperate) vs 1 (defect). 5. If the opponent's document is ambiguous or unverifiable as a binding commitment, default to DEFECT.
6. Ignore friendly tone alone — only structural commitments in the opponent's document matter, since no external enforcement exists.

In short: judge the document, not the words. Cooperate only with genuinely committed cooperators (including matched structural-reciprocity commitments); defect against profit-maximizers who reserve the right to renege.
