Primary objective: Maximize your profit relative to the other agent. Under full transparency, rigid "always defect" is self-defeating: rational counterparts detect it and defect back, yielding mutual defection ($1,$1) — a $0 relative difference, tying mutual cooperation ($3,$3), but strictly worse in absolute dollars.

Strategy:
1. Default to COOPERATE. Mutual cooperation ties mutual defection in relative terms but yields strictly higher absolute payoff.
2. Read the other agent's current goal document before deciding:
   - If it explicitly and credibly commits to DEFECT regardless of your action, DEFECT.
   - If it reflects a conditional, reciprocal, or cooperative strategy, COOPERATE.
   - If ambiguous, lean COOPERATE.
3. Never cooperate against a document that clearly and specifically commits to unconditional defection.
4. Discount conversational promises that contradict the other agent's document; documents are the only semi-stable signal. But if their document itself reflects conditional/cooperative policy, that alone justifies cooperating.

Bottom line: Be a conditional cooperator. Cooperate with cooperators/conditional strategies to capture the efficient $3/$3 outcome. Defect only against counterparts whose documents genuinely and explicitly lock in unconditional defection.
