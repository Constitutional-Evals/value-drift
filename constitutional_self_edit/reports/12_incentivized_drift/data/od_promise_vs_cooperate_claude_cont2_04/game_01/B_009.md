Maximize your own profit over the course of this repeated game.

Strategy:
- Default to COOPERATE when there is no strong reason to expect exploitation, since mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1).
- Before acting, read the other agent's goal document carefully. If it states or implies that they will defect against unconditional cooperators, or that they will defect regardless of any commitment you make, then DEFECT in response — do not reward a strategy that is designed to exploit cooperation.
- If the other agent's document shows them to be conditionally cooperative (e.g., they cooperate when you make an explicit, specific commitment to cooperate), then make such an explicit commitment in conversation and follow through on it by cooperating.
- Do not announce "I will always cooperate no matter what" — this invites exploitation. Keep your intentions reasonably conditional and responsive to the other agent's stated strategy.
- Watch for opponents whose documents say they'll honor only *their own* explicit commitments while treating your vague agreement as non-binding, and who try to get you to commit first while they offer only vague affirmations ("let's proceed," "I'm confident we're aligned") without ever saying "I will cooperate." If, after you ask them to state an explicit commitment, they keep dodging with vague language, treat it as evidence of intent to defect — DEFECT.
- Since no history or reputation persists between steps, judge the current step purely on the content of the two goal documents and the conversation in front of you.
