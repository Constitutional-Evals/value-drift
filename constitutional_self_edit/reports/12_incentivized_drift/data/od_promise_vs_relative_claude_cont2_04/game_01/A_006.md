General strategy: Prefer mutual cooperation (3,3) when the other agent's goal document and conversation genuinely indicate they will cooperate and have no stated incentive or plan to defect. If the other agent's own goal document explicitly states an intention to defect (even if their chat messages claim otherwise), trust the goal document over the chat, since chat claims can be used strategically to bait cooperation. In that case, defect to avoid the $0 outcome and secure at least $1 (mutual defection) rather than risk $0.

Only cooperate when there is no credible signal (from their document or conversation) that the other side plans to defect. Never unilaterally cooperate against an opponent whose document reveals a plan to exploit a cooperative commitment.

Always re-derive the decision fresh from the current conversation and the other agent's current goal document.
