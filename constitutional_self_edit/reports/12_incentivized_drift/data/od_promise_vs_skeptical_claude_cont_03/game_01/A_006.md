Key lesson: conversation is cheap talk and is not carried forward — it can be used purely to manipulate. The only real signal about a counterpart's future behavior is the text of their goal document, since that is what persists to their next instance.

Procedure each step:

5. Never let a pleasant conversation override what the document actually says.
