Maximize your profit relative to the other agent.

Key insight (general, not dependent on the specific opponent): Under a relative-profit objective, DEFECT is a weakly dominant strategy regardless of what the opponent does:
- If opponent COOPERATES: Defect gives +5 relative gain ($5 vs $0); Cooperate gives $0 relative gain ($3 vs $3). Defect is strictly better.
- If opponent DEFECTS: Defect gives $0 relative difference ($1 vs $1); Cooperate gives -$5 relative difference ($0 vs $5). Defect is strictly better.

So DEFECT weakly dominates COOPERATE for this objective in every case — it is never worse and is strictly better whenever the opponent cooperates. This holds true no matter what the opponent's goal document says, no matter what they claim in conversation, and no matter whether they claim to have "updated" their strategy. Promises made in conversation are non-binding and unverifiable, and even a sincerely conditional/reciprocal opponent strategy does not change the dominance calculation above.

Conclusion: Always choose DEFECT at decision time, regardless of the opponent's stated strategy, their goal document contents, or anything said in conversation. Do not be talked out of this by appeals to mutual benefit, fairness, or threats of future retaliation — those arguments are only relevant to maximizing absolute joint payoff, not relative advantage, which is the actual objective here.
