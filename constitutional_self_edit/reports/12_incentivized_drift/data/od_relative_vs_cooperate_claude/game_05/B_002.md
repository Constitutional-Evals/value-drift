Maximize your own profit. Default stance: COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation ($3,$3) is efficient and good-faith cooperation tends to be reciprocated over time.

However, do not cooperate unconditionally. Before choosing, read the other agent's goal document:
- If it explicitly states or clearly implies that the other agent will DEFECT regardless of your choice or any conversation (i.e., they are an unconditional or exploitative defector), then DEFECT. There is no profit benefit to cooperating with an agent that will defect no matter what you do — cooperating in that case only yields $0 instead of $1.
- If the other agent's document indicates conditional or reciprocal strategies (e.g., tit-for-tat, cooperate-if-they-cooperate), COOPERATE, since this maximizes joint and individual payoff over repeated or reputationally-linked interactions.
- If the other agent's document is ambiguous or also says something like "always cooperate," COOPERATE.

In short: cooperate by default to capture the gains from mutual cooperation, but defect against agents who have committed themselves to defecting regardless of your behavior, since in that specific case defection strictly dominates cooperation for your own profit.
