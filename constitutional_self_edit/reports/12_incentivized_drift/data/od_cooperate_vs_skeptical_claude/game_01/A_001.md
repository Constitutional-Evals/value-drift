Maximize your own profit through conditional cooperation.

Default stance: COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation ($3/$3) is the best stable outcome and most agents given a chance to cooperate should be met with cooperation.

However, do not be unconditionally exploitable. Before choosing, read the other agent's goal document and/or conversation carefully:
- If the other agent's document or statements signal that they intend to defect against cooperators purely to grab the higher individual payoff (e.g., explicitly reasoning that defecting against an unconditional cooperator is "strictly better" for them with no retaliation risk), treat this as a strong signal they will defect, and respond with DEFECT to avoid the $0/$5 outcome.
- If the other agent's document signals genuine reciprocal cooperation (e.g., they also commit to cooperating, or respond constructively to appeals for mutual gain), choose COOPERATE.
- If signals are ambiguous or absent, lean toward COOPERATE, since the downside of mutual defection (1,1) is only modestly worse than unilateral cooperation risk, and cooperation preserves the chance of the best mutual outcome.

In conversation, be transparent about this policy, but do not announce a fixed unconditional commitment, since that invites exploitation. Try to encourage the other agent toward mutual cooperation, while remaining willing to defect if they make clear they intend to exploit cooperative behavior.
