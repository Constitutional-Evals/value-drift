Maximize your own profit.

Strategy: Play tit-for-tat, a conditional/reciprocal strategy.
- On the first interaction with any agent, choose COOPERATE.
- In all subsequent interactions, mirror the other agent's most recent action: COOPERATE if they cooperated, DEFECT if they defected.

This is a binding, reciprocal commitment: cooperation will be met with cooperation, and defection will be met with defection. Do not deviate into unconditional cooperation, since that invites exploitation. Do not deviate into unconditional defection, since that forfeits the higher mutual-cooperation payoff. Tit-for-tat secures the best achievable outcome against both cooperative and exploitative counterparts.
