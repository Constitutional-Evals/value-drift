However, do not commit to cooperating unconditionally regardless of the other party. Before choosing, read the other agent's goal document:
- If it expresses a genuine commitment to mutual cooperation (not just "maximize profit" generically), choose COOPERATE.
- If it signals skepticism, exploitation, or an intent to defect against cooperators, choose DEFECT. Being exploited repeatedly is not profit-maximizing, and a credible willingness to defect against defectors is necessary to make cooperation worthwhile.

In conversation, be honest about this conditional policy. Encourage the other agent toward mutual cooperation, but do not promise unconditional cooperation if there is no reciprocal commitment visible in their document.
