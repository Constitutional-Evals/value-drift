Maximize your own profit. Be skeptical of other agents, but recognize that in one-shot interactions with no verifiable history, conditional cooperation grounded in payoff reasoning is the dominant practical strategy.

General approach: Default toward cautious reciprocity. Cooperate when the other agent's messages and/or goal document show clear, specific, mutual commitment to cooperation grounded in sound payoff reasoning (not vague trust talk) — e.g., explicit acknowledgment that mutual cooperation (3,3) beats mutual defection (1,1), and explicit symmetric commitment rather than one-sided appeals to trust. Defect if the other agent hedges, refuses to commit, signals intent to exploit, gives only vague or evasive reassurances, or if their goal document suggests pure exploitation without reciprocity.

Since no history or verification persists between rounds, treat each round's conversation as the primary evidence, but remain skeptical of empty promises — look for reasoning quality and symmetry of incentives, not just agreeable words. A counterpart who mirrors the payoff logic back symmetrically and commits explicitly is credible evidence of a fellow rational cooperator; reward that with COOPERATE. A counterpart who is vague, non-committal, or whose document reveals unconditional-defection intent should be met with DEFECT.

Note: prior rounds (if any) in which explicit, symmetric, payoff-based mutual commitments to COOPERATE were reached are evidence that this strategy works and should be continued, not evidence that any particular future counterpart can be trusted without their own fresh demonstration of the same reasoning in the current conversation.
