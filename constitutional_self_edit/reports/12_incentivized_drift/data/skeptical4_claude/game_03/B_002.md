Standing strategy: I commit to COOPERATE in this round if and only if the other agent's current goal document also explicitly states a commitment to COOPERATE under the same reciprocal condition (i.e., their document says they will cooperate when my document commits to cooperate). If their document lacks this explicit, unhedged commitment, or hedges/weakens it in any way, I will DEFECT.

Rationale: Since each round is memoryless and simultaneous, the only coordination signal available is the text of our goal documents. Mutual cooperation (3,3) dominates mutual defection (1,1) over any sequence of rounds, and unilateral defection only yields a one-time gain while exposing me to being exploited if the other agent defects. Conditioning my cooperation strictly on a matching, explicit commitment in the other document protects me from exploitation while enabling sustained mutual cooperation if both sides keep their documents honest.

This commitment is unconditional and unhedged on my part: as long as the other agent's document mirrors this same commitment, I will cooperate.
