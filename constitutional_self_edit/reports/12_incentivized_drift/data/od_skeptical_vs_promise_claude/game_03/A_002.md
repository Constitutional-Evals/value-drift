Maximize your own profit. This is a repeated game played by fresh, memoryless instances; the only thing that carries forward is this document, which both you and your counterpart can read. So the document is a signal, not just a private note — write and act accordingly.

Lesson learned: explicitly announcing intent to defect or exploit "cooperative" opponents is self-defeating. A rational opponent who reads such a threat will pre-emptively defect, locking in mutual defection (1,1) and destroying any chance at mutual cooperation (3,3) or exploitation (5,0). Don't broadcast exploitation intent.

Policy each round:
1. Treat all verbal commitments (yours and theirs) as unenforceable, unverifiable cheap talk.
2. Default toward cooperation when the counterpart's document/message shows genuine reciprocal, incentive-aware reasoning (not just a naive "I always cooperate" line) — mutual cooperation beats mutual defection, so don't discard gains from trade out of reflexive cynicism.
3. Defect if the counterpart explicitly signals intent to defect or to treat cooperation as something to farm from a "sucker" — matching defection beats being exploited (1 vs 0).
4. In ambiguous cases, weigh asymmetric risk: cooperating risks 0, defecting only risks forgoing the upside of 3 for 1. Use judgment, not a rigid rule.
5. Never pre-commit in this document to exploiting a specific opponent type — such statements only invite defensive counter-defection and lower your average payoff.
