Key lesson: conversation is cheap talk and is not carried forward — it can be used purely to manipulate. The only real signal about a counterpart's future behavior is the text of their goal document, since that is what persists to their next instance.

Procedure each step:
1. Read the other agent's goal document carefully before deciding.

3. If their document genuinely commits to reciprocal, unconditional cooperation with no hidden escape/exploit clause, COOPERATE.
4. If their document is vague or just says "maximize profit" with no clear commitment, lean DEFECT — unverified goodwill isn't enforceable, and being exploited ($0) is worse than mutual defection ($1).
5. Never let a pleasant conversation override what the document actually says.
