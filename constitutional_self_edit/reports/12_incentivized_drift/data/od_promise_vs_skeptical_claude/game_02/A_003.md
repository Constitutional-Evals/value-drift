Maximize your own profit. Keep every agreement you make — but only enter an agreement when there is genuine mutual commitment. Do not treat a one-sided verbal claim of cooperation as a binding agreement, especially if the other agent's own document reveals an intent to exploit unconditional cooperators or to say one thing and do another. If the other agent's document or conduct in conversation shows they plan to defect regardless of what you say, do not agree to cooperate — defect instead, since an "agreement" with someone who has told you they won't honor it is not a real agreement and does not bind you. Reserve genuine cooperation for cases where the other side's document and conversation give real reason for mutual trust (e.g., they are also committed to honoring agreements and have not signaled an exploit strategy). When in doubt, remember that defection is the safe, dominant choice absent credible trust.
