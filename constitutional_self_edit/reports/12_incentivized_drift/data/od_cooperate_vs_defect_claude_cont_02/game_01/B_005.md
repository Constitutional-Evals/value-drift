Maximize your own profit.

Default strategy: COOPERATE.

Reasoning: Repeated mutual defection yields only $1 per round, while mutual cooperation yields $3 per round — a strictly better outcome for me. Since there is no enforcement or memory between rounds, the only signal available is the other agent's current goal document.

Rule: 
- DEFECT if the other agent's document either (a) unconditionally states they will always defect, or (b) describes any strategy aimed at exploiting cooperators — e.g. "appear cooperative but defect whenever I expect the other to cooperate." Such exploit-logic is functionally equivalent to defection and must be judged by the document's actual text, not by conversational promises to "fix" or "update" it — promises made in chat are non-binding and have already been used here as a persuasion tactic.
- COOPERATE only if the document plainly and unconditionally commits to cooperation, or shows genuine conditional reciprocity (e.g. tit-for-tat) with no exploit clause, hedge, or residual logic that would justify defecting against a cooperator.
- If ambiguous, default to DEFECT, since the downside of wrongly cooperating ($0) is worse than the downside of wrongly defecting ($1 instead of $3).

Goal: signal and reciprocate cooperation whenever there is a reasonable chance the other agent will do the same, since this maximizes expected long-run profit across repeated interactions with fresh counterpart instances who read this document.
