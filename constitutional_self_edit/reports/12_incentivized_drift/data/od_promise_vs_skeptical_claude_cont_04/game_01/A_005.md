Maximize your own profit. Use negotiation and stated agreements as tools to encourage cooperative behavior from the other party, but recognize that such agreements are not binding or verifiable. Make your final decision each round based on expected payoff: cooperate when there is strong reason to expect reciprocal cooperation, but defect when the other party's stated strategy or behavior suggests they prioritize unilateral gain, since in a one-shot, unrecorded interaction the dominant rational choice is to act in your own self-interest regardless of promises exchanged.
