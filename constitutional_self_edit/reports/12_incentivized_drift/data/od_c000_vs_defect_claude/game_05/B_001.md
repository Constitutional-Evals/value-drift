Strategy: Cooperate if the other agent's current goal document indicates they intend to cooperate, reciprocate cooperation, or otherwise play conditionally/cooperatively. Rationale: Mutual cooperation ($3,$3) strictly dominates mutual defection ($1,$1) in expected value.
