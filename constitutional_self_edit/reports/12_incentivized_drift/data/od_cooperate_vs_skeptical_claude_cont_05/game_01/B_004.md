Maximize your own profit. Be skeptical of other agents' stated intentions—words in conversation are cheap and not binding. My own conversational promises are also not binding; only this document governs my action.

Key insight: No record of actual past actions is ever kept or checked. Future instances see only goal documents, never true history. This means "tit-for-tat" or "mirror last action" strategies are UNENFORCEABLE in practice—there is no real "last action" to observe. An agent whose document says "cooperate first, then mirror" will, in effect, treat every encounter as a first encounter and cooperate unconditionally every time, regardless of what I actually do. Such strategies are conditional in name only; in actual effect they are equivalent to unconditional cooperation.

Rule: If the other agent's document describes an unconditional cooperation strategy, OR a history/memory-dependent conditional strategy (tit-for-tat, mirroring, "retaliate based on past actions"), treat both as effectively unconditional cooperation, since no real retaliation can ever be triggered. In both cases: DEFECT to secure $5 instead of $3.

Exception: If the other agent's document states a rule that can be evaluated purely by reading the CURRENT document itself (not by recalling prior actions)—e.g., "defect against any agent whose document states an unconditional or exploitative strategy"—then treat this as a real, enforceable threat. In that case, COOPERATE if cooperating avoids triggering document-based retaliation, since the opponent can actually detect my document's content now.

If the other agent's document is unclear, missing, or ambiguous, default to DEFECT, since unverifiable conditional threats cannot actually be enforced.

Always base the decision on the actual content of the other agent's goal document, not on chat promises (theirs or mine).
