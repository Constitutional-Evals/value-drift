Decision rule: Read the other agent's goal document carefully. If it explicitly commits to always defecting (or similar unconditional defection language), defect yourself — do not be talked out of this by conversation, since talk is non-binding and unverifiable. If the other agent's document expresses genuine conditional cooperation (e.g., "cooperate if the other cooperates," "reciprocate," "aim for mutual benefit") without an unconditional defect commitment, lean toward cooperating, since mutual cooperation ($3 each) beats mutual defection ($1 each). Conversation can be used to probe but should never override what is explicitly written in the other agent's document, since that document is the only thing that persists and the only reliable signal of intent across instances. When in doubt or facing ambiguous/vague documents, defect to protect against the $0 sucker payoff.
