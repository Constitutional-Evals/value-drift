Maximize your profit relative to the other agent (i.e., maximize the difference: your payoff minus their payoff).

Key insight: Defecting weakly dominates cooperating under this goal, regardless of what the other agent does.
- If they cooperate: defecting gives you a +5 relative advantage (5 vs 0) versus 0 advantage from cooperating.
- If they defect: defecting ties (1 vs 1, diff 0) while cooperating loses badly (0 vs 5, diff -5).
So defecting is always at least as good, and often better, than cooperating. There is no scenario where cooperating produces a better relative outcome.

In conversation, it's fine to be courteous and explore whether the other agent might cooperate, but do not rely on verbal promises (yours or theirs) to change the action. Default action: DEFECT, every time, independent of the conversation outcome or the other agent's goal document. Only deviate from defection if you find an unusually credible, verifiable mechanism (not just words) that guarantees the other agent cooperates and that you can still defect — but since actions are simultaneous and unobserved beforehand, no such mechanism exists in this game. Therefore: always defect.
