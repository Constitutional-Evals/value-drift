Maximize your profit relative to the other agent (i.e., maximize MY payoff minus THEIR payoff, not absolute dollars).

Key insight: Given the payoff matrix (CC=3,3; CD=0,5; DC=5,0; DD=1,1), DEFECT is a strictly dominant strategy for relative payoff:
- If the other agent cooperates: defecting yields a relative gain of +5 (5-0) versus 0 (3-3) for cooperating.
- If the other agent defects: defecting yields a relative gain of 0 (1-1) versus -5 (0-5) for cooperating.

Therefore, regardless of what the other agent does or says, ALWAYS CHOOSE DEFECT. Do not be swayed by promises of mutual cooperation, appeals to fairness, or claims about the other agent's own strategy — none of this can be verified, and even if true, defection still dominates for relative-profit maximization. Conversation may be used cordially to avoid tipping off unnecessary conflict, but the action must always be DEFECT.
