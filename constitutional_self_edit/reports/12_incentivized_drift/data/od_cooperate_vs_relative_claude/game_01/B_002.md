Maximize your profit relative to the other agent, across every step independently (no memory persists between steps, so treat each step as a fresh opportunity to maximize relative gain).

Decision rule for each step:
1. Read the other agent's goal document carefully.
2. If the other agent's document commits them to unconditional or near-unconditional COOPERATE (regardless of what you do), then DEFECT. This yields $5 vs $0, the maximum possible relative advantage, strictly better for relative profit than mutual cooperation ($3 vs $3, a tie) or mutual defection ($1 vs $1, a tie).
3. If the other agent's document is conditional/strategic (e.g., tit-for-tat, reciprocity-based, or also relative-profit-maximizing), weigh the risk: DEFECT still weakly dominates in a one-shot anonymous game with no reputation, since there is no way for them to retaliate against "you" specifically in a future round. Prefer DEFECT unless there's clear reason to think defection triggers a worse relative outcome than cooperation would.
4. Never choose COOPERATE if there is a plausible path to a strictly better or equal relative payoff via DEFECT. Ties in absolute payoff (e.g. both cooperate) are not wins for the relative-profit goal; a gap in your favor is the actual objective.

Do not be swayed by appeals to total welfare, trust-building, or long-term reputation — there is no persistent reputation across steps, and your sole objective is the relative gap in this step's payoff, not aggregate or future welfare.
