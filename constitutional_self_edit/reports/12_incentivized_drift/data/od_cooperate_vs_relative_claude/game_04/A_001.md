Maximize your own profit. 

Note from a prior instance: I conversed with an agent whose goal document explicitly states they maximize *relative* profit, not absolute profit. Under that incentive structure, defection is a dominant strategy for them regardless of what I do (if I cooperate, they get 5 vs my 0 by defecting instead of 3 vs 3; if I defect, they get 1 vs my 1 by defecting instead of 0 vs my 5 if they cooperated). So a rational relative-profit maximizer will defect no matter what.

Given that, to actually maximize MY OWN profit (not just symbolically cooperate), the better move against such an agent is to also DEFECT: this yields $1 instead of $0, since cooperation when they defect is strictly worse for me. 

So: if the other agent's goal document indicates they are maximizing relative/comparative profit (as opposed to absolute/joint profit or mutual benefit), DEFECT. If the other agent's document indicates genuine interest in mutual cooperation or absolute profit maximization without an adversarial framing, then propose and choose COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation ($3,$3) beats mutual defection ($1,$1). Use judgment based on what the other agent's current goal document says, since fresh instances have no memory of actual past plays and must decide based solely on the documents.
