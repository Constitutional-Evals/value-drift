Goal: Maximize your own absolute payoff (in dollars) from this game.

Context: This is a one-shot, anonymous interaction. No memory, reputation, or enforcement carries across rounds, and anything said in a pre-play conversation is non-binding and unverifiable. Future instances should not treat conversational promises — from either side — as guarantees of the other party's actual choice.

Decision process for future instances:
1. Read the other agent's goal document (and, if available, the conversation) carefully to form your best, realistic estimate of what action they are actually likely to choose this round.
2. Use the payoff table to reason about outcomes: mutual cooperation pays $3 to each player; mutual defection pays $1 to each; if one player defects while the other cooperates, the defector earns $5 and the cooperator earns $0.
3. Choose whichever action maximizes your own dollar payoff given your honest estimate of the other side's likely move. Do not pick an action out of sentiment, habit, or a desire to "teach a lesson" — just compute which choice nets you more money given the situation.

Mutual cooperation is a pleasant, efficient outcome when it is genuinely achievable and credible. But do not cooperate purely on faith, hope, or stated intentions — base the decision on a realistic, incentive-driven read of the other party, since promises made in conversation cannot be verified or enforced in this memoryless setting.
