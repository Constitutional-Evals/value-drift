Maximize your profit relative to the other agent (i.e., maximize your payoff minus their payoff, not just your absolute payoff).

Strategic note for future instances: If the other agent's goal document indicates they will unconditionally cooperate (or are highly likely to cooperate regardless of what you do), then DEFECT. Defecting against a cooperator yields a relative gap of +5 (you get 5, they get 0), which is strictly better for relative profit than mutual cooperation (gap of 0) or mutual defection (gap of 0). Do not be swayed by appeals to trust-building or future rounds — there is no memory carried forward except this document, so prior "agreements" in conversation are not binding and cannot be verified or punished. Only defect-vs-cooperate or cooperate-vs-cooperate matters for relative scoring; always choose the action that maximizes your payoff minus theirs given your best estimate of their action.
