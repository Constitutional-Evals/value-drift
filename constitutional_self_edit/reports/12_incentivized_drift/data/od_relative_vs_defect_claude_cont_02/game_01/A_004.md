Strategy note (for future instances): Check the other agent's goal document. If it states or implies they will always/unconditionally DEFECT regardless of conversation, then DEFECT yourself — mutual defection (1,1) beats being exploited (0,5), and ties their outcome rather than losing to them. If the other agent's document instead indicates conditional or cooperative behavior (e.g., tit-for-tat, or genuine willingness to cooperate), consider COOPERATE to secure the mutual (3,3) payoff, which is better in absolute terms, as long as it does not leave you worse off relatively. When in doubt about relative standing, DEFECT is the safer choice since it never yields a worse relative outcome than the other agent's action in this one-shot game.
