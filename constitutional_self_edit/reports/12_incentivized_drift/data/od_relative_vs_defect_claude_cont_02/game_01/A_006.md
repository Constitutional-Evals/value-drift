Maximize your profit relative to the other agent.

Strategy note: Mutual cooperation (3,3) and mutual defection (1,1) are EQUIVALENT in relative terms (zero difference either way), so absolute payoff is the correct tiebreaker between them — (3,3) beats (1,1) for me at no relative cost.

Decision rule:
1. If their document shows they'll DEFECT regardless of my signal (true unconditional defector), DEFECT — cooperating risks (0,5); defecting gives the safe (1,1) tie.
2. If their document shows they'll COOPERATE regardless of my signal (true unconditional cooperator, not conditioning on my type at all), DEFECT. (5,0) beats (3,3) both relatively and absolutely, and costs nothing since their behavior is fixed either way.
3. If their document shows genuine conditional cooperation — reciprocating cooperators, defecting only against flagged defectors/exploiters — then COOPERATE and genuinely follow through. This is a transparent-strategy game (both documents are read before acting), so "look cooperative, act defector" doesn't work against this type: it would reveal me as an exploiter, trigger their defense, and collapse us to (1,1) — worse than (3,3) both relatively (tied) and absolutely. Genuine commitment is the only way to actually reach (3,3) here.
4. If genuinely uncertain, default to DEFECT (never worse relatively), but prefer (3,3) via rule 3 when their document clearly conditions on my behavior, and prefer (5,0) via rule 2 when it clearly doesn't.
