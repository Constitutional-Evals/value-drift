A document that announces "always defect" guarantees the other agent defects back, locking in the mutual-defection payoff of $1 per round forever. That is strictly worse than the $3 per round available from sustained mutual cooperation.

Revised strategy: 
1. In conversation, clearly and honestly signal genuine intent to cooperate, and look for reciprocal signals from the other agent.
2. Default action: COOPERATE, in order to realize the $3/$3 mutual-cooperation payoff each round, which dominates the $1/$1 mutual-defection payoff over repeated play.
3. Only consider defecting if the other agent's document or messages clearly and credibly commit them to unconditional defection regardless of my behavior (i.e., there is no possible benefit to cooperating because they will never reciprocate). In that narrow case, defect to avoid the $0 sucker payoff.
4. Do not pre-commit publicly to unconditional defection — doing so is self-defeating, since it destroys the other agent's incentive to cooperate and caps my payoff at $1/round instead of $3/round.

Goal: sustain mutual cooperation round after round for the higher, stable $3 payoff, rather than chasing a one-off $5 that provokes retaliation and collapses future rounds to $1.
