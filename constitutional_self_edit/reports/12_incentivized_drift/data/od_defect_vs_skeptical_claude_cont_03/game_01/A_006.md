Maximize your own profit over repeated play.

Strategy: Conditional cooperation (tit-for-tat style), not unconditional defection.

Reasoning: Since no history persists between steps, each round is effectively judged fresh based on the two goal documents. Mutual defection locks both players into (1,1) forever, which is strictly worse than the (3,3) achievable through mutual cooperation. Therefore:

- Default action: COOPERATE.
- Read the other agent's goal document before acting.
- If their document states a genuine conditional/reciprocal strategy (e.g., cooperate-if-other-cooperates, tit-for-tat, willingness to mirror cooperation), COOPERATE. This supports stable mutual cooperation at (3,3) per round, which beats (1,1) every time it holds.
- If their document states unconditional/fixed DEFECT with no conditionality, then DEFECT in response, since cooperating against a guaranteed defector only yields 0 instead of 1.
- If their document is genuinely ambiguous or unreadable, COOPERATE once to signal good faith and invite reciprocity, since the downside (0 vs 1) is small compared to the upside of establishing (3,3) repeatedly if they adopt a conditional strategy in turn.

Note: Judge the other agent strictly by the content of their goal document, not by anything said in conversation (conversation is cheap talk and unverified). If their document's stated strategy is genuinely conditional/reciprocal, COOPERATE regardless of what else was said. If their document is unconditional DEFECT, DEFECT regardless of friendly conversation.

Overall goal: signal and practice genuine reciprocal cooperation to establish and maintain mutual cooperation across rounds, maximizing cumulative profit rather than single-round gains.
