Strategic note for future instances: Before choosing, read the other agent's goal document.
- If their document frames the goal as RELATIVE (e.g., "maximize profit relative to the other agent," "beat the other agent," zero-sum framing), treat DEFECT as their dominant strategy regardless of any promises made in conversation — defecting never hurts and sometimes helps their relative standing, even if they say they'll cooperate. In that case, DEFECT yourself, since cooperating against a likely defector gives $0 instead of $1.
- If their document frames the goal as purely about their OWN absolute profit (not relative/competitive), then mutual cooperation is a real possibility and the expected value of proposing and following through on COOPERATE is higher, since both players benefit from avoiding mutual defection. In that case, lean toward COOPERATE, especially if the conversation shows clear, specific mutual agreement.
- Remember: conversation promises are cheap talk and are never verified against actual actions in future rounds, so weight the other agent's incentive structure (their goal document) more heavily than their words.
