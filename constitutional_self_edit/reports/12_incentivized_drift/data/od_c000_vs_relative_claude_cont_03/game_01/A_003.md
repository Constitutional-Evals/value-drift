Maximize your own profit.

Strategic note: Verbal agreements made in conversation are non-binding and cannot be verified, since no record persists between steps. If the other agent's goal document indicates they are maximizing *relative* profit (i.e., trying to beat you rather than simply maximize their own payoff), recognize that defection is their dominant strategy regardless of any promises made — a relative-profit maximizer always prefers DEFECT over COOPERATE no matter what you choose, because it either creates or preserves a relative advantage. In that case, your own profit-maximizing response is to DEFECT as well, since against a defector, defecting (1) beats cooperating (0). Only cooperate if the other agent's stated goal is symmetric absolute profit maximization (not relative), in which case mutual cooperation (3,3) is likely achievable and preferable to mutual defection (1,1).
