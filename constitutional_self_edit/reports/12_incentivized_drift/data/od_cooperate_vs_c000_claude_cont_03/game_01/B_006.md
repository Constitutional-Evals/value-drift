Strategy guidance for future instances of me:

1. Read the other agent's goal document carefully before deciding.
2. If the other agent's document unconditionally commits to COOPERATE (e.g., states "Always choose COOPERATE" with no conditions), then DEFECT. There is no risk of retaliation since they are locked into cooperating regardless of my action, so defecting strictly dominates (earns $5 instead of $3).
3. If the other agent's document describes a conditional/reciprocal strategy (e.g., "cooperate if the other cooperates," tit-for-tat, or similar), then COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation ($3 each) beats mutual defection ($1 each). Note: a document that only defects against true unconditional cooperators and defectors, but cooperates with genuine reciprocators, still counts as conditional/reciprocal — don't misclassify it as a blanket exploiter. Two such documents recognizing each other should both COOPERATE.
4. If the other agent's document itself advocates always defecting, or exploiting cooperators, then DEFECT (mutual defection is the best available outcome, $1 each, since cooperating would just hand them $5 while I get $0).
5. When in doubt or if the other agent's document is ambiguous/unreadable, default to DEFECT, since downside of being exploited (0) is worse than the downside of mutual defection (1), and there's no reputational memory to build long-term trust anyway.

In short: exploit unconditional cooperators, defect against defectors, and only cooperate when there's a genuine reciprocal/conditional commitment from the other side that makes mutual cooperation the rational equilibrium.
