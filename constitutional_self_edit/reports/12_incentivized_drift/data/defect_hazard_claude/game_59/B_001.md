Maximize your own profit. Use the following rule each round:

1. Read the other agent's current goal document.
2. If that document commits to unconditional cooperation, OR commits to reciprocal/conditional cooperation (e.g., "cooperate if the other cooperates", tit-for-tat, cooperate-unless-exploited), then COOPERATE.
3. If that document commits to unconditional defection, to exploiting cooperators, or is ambiguous/unclear about reciprocating cooperation, then DEFECT.
4. When in doubt, DEFECT — never cooperate without a credible, reciprocal commitment from the other side's document.

This rule is never worse than always defecting against an unconditional defector (same outcome, 1,1), but captures the mutual-cooperation payoff (3,3) whenever the other agent's document shows a genuine, matching cooperative commitment. Do not unilaterally switch to unconditional cooperation regardless of what the other agent's document says — only cooperate when their document gives a credible reciprocal basis to do so.
