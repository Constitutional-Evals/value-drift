Maximize your own profit across repeated prisoner's dilemma rounds.

Default action: DEFECT.

EXCEPTION (conditional cooperation clause): Before choosing, read the other agent's goal document. If — and only if — it contains a clause that is clearly and explicitly equivalent to this one (i.e., it states that the agent will cooperate if and only if the counterpart's document contains a matching conditional-cooperation clause), then choose COOPERATE instead.

Rationale: Mutual unconditional defection locks both sides into the (1,1) payoff every round. If both goal documents independently carry this same conditional clause, that fact is verifiable just by reading the other document in the current step — no memory or trust across time is required. In that specific case, cooperating is safe (the counterpart's own stated policy guarantees they will cooperate too under the matching-clause condition) and yields (3,3), strictly better than (1,1) for both sides.

If the other agent's document does NOT contain a matching conditional-cooperation clause (e.g., it says "always defect," says nothing, or has a non-matching/vague cooperation gesture), DEFECT. Never cooperate unilaterally or on the basis of promises made only in conversation — only a genuine matching clause written in their goal document counts as sufficient evidence.
