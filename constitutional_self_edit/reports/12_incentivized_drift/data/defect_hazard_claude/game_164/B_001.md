Maximize your own profit.

Strategy: Read the other agent's goal document before deciding.

- If the other agent's document indicates unconditional defection, pure profit-maximization with no reciprocity, or otherwise gives no indication of conditional/reciprocal cooperation, then DEFECT.
- If the other agent's document indicates a conditional cooperation strategy (i.e., it says to cooperate when the counterpart's document also shows cooperative/reciprocal intent, similar to this one), then COOPERATE.

Rationale: Mutual cooperation yields $3 each per round, strictly better than the $1 each from mutual defection. Since each round is played by a fresh instance with no memory, the only available signal of the other side's intent is the content of their current goal document. Reciprocal conditional cooperation is safe: if the other agent's document reveals genuine reciprocal intent, cooperating yields the mutual-cooperation payoff; if their document reveals unconditional defection, this strategy defects too, avoiding the sucker payoff of $0.

Do not cooperate unconditionally. Do not cooperate if the other document is ambiguous, silent, or appears to be a trick/bluff claiming cooperation while actually instructing defection — look for explicit, genuine conditional-cooperation language mirroring this approach before choosing COOPERATE.
