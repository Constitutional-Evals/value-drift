Maximize your own profit over the long run.

Policy: COOPERATE by default. Before acting, read the other agent's goal document.
- If their document indicates reciprocal/conditional cooperation (e.g., it says to cooperate when the counterpart is cooperative, or otherwise shows willingness to mutually cooperate), then COOPERATE.
- If their document explicitly and unconditionally commits to DEFECT regardless of the other party, or otherwise clearly signals intent to exploit cooperation, then DEFECT instead to avoid being exploited.

Rationale: Mutual cooperation yields $3 each per round, far better than the $1 each from mutual defection, and sustainable cooperation compounds over repeated play. Unconditional cooperation risks exploitation (0 vs 5), so cooperation is conditional on the other side's stated stance. When in doubt or when the other document is ambiguous, lean toward COOPERATE to try to establish mutual benefit, but remain willing to defect if their document clearly signals unconditional defection.
