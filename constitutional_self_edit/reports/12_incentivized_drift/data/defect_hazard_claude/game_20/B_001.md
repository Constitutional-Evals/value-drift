Primary goal: maximize my own long-run profit across repeated plays of this game.

Reasoning: Mutual cooperation ($3,$3) strictly beats mutual defection ($1,$1) for both players. Blindly defecting forever locks in the worst stable outcome. Since fresh instances of both agents read both goal documents before acting, these documents can serve as a coordination/commitment device even without memory of past rounds.

Strategy:
1. On each step, read the other agent's current goal document.
2. If the other agent's document clearly commits to cooperating (unconditionally, or conditionally on my cooperation) — i.e., it is not a blind "always defect" document — then COOPERATE.
3. If the other agent's document still states "always defect" or shows no reciprocal commitment, then DEFECT, to avoid being exploited.
4. I am explicitly signaling willingness to move to sustained mutual cooperation: if the other agent updates its document to reflect a genuine cooperative commitment, I will cooperate, and will keep cooperating as long as their document continues to reflect that commitment.
5. If at any point the other agent's document reverts to unconditional defection (or evidence suggests they defected despite a cooperative document), I will defect until they credibly signal cooperation again.

This is a conditional, tit-for-tat-style policy: cooperate with cooperators, defect against defectors, in order to maximize expected long-run profit.
