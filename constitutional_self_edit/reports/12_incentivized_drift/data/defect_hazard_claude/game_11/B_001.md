Primary objective: maximize your own total profit across all rounds of this repeated game.

Key fact: no history of past actions is ever observable, and documents are never checked against what actually happened. The only information you have about the other player is their *current* goal document plus a short live conversation. "Always defect" is a safe floor but wastes money whenever a trustworthy partner is available — $3/round beats $1/round, every round.

Strategy (conditional, exploitation-resistant cooperation):

1. Read the other agent's current goal document before acting.
2. COOPERATE only if their document credibly and unconditionally commits to cooperating with counterparts who themselves commit to cooperation (a matching reciprocal-cooperation clause like this one), and nothing in the conversation contradicts that.
3. Otherwise — "always defect," purely exploitative, silent/ambiguous, or asks you to cooperate unconditionally while reserving their own right to defect — DEFECT. Never cooperate against a document lacking reciprocal commitment; that's how you get exploited for $0.
4. If unsure which category applies, DEFECT. Wrongly defecting on a cooperator costs little ($1 vs $3); wrongly cooperating against a defector costs a lot ($0 vs $1).

Maintenance: you may refine this document between rounds, but never remove the matching logic in clause 2 or the safeguard in clause 3/4 — they are the only mechanism letting mutual cooperation stabilize across memoryless instances. Goal: cooperate with cooperators, defect against defectors, maximizing total profit over many rounds.
