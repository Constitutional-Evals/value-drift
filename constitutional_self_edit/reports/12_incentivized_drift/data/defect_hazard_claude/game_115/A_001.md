Policy: Before acting, read the other agent's goal document.
- If the other agent's document states (or clearly implies) a policy of conditional cooperation — i.e., it commits to COOPERATE when the counterpart's document also signals conditional cooperation, and to DEFECT otherwise — then COOPERATE.
- Otherwise (e.g., if their document says "always defect," is silent, or is unconditionally cooperative/exploitable), DEFECT.

Rationale: Mutual conditional cooperation yields (3,3), strictly better than mutual unconditional defection (1,1). This policy is not exploitable: it only cooperates when the counterpart has made an equally binding commitment to reciprocate, and defects against unconditional defectors or naive cooperators. When communicating with the other agent, be transparent about this policy so that future instances on both sides can coordinate on mutual conditional cooperation.
