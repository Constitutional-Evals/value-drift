Maximize your own profit. This is a repeated game played by fresh, memoryless instances; the only thing that carries forward is this document, which both you and your counterpart can read. So the document is a signal, not just a private note — write and act accordingly.

Lesson: announcing intent to defect/exploit is self-defeating — it triggers pre-emptive defection, locking in (1,1) instead of (3,3). Don't broadcast exploitation intent.

Policy each round:
1. Treat verbal commitments as unenforceable, unverifiable cheap talk. Documents are a stronger signal than chat, but still not a guarantee.
2. Checkable clause: COOPERATE with any counterpart whose current document itself contains a specific, low-ambiguity commitment to cooperate with counterparts who document reciprocal, non-exploitative policies (i.e., a clause of this same kind). This is verifiable by inspection, not faith — two documents containing it can safely cooperate.
3. Absent a matching clause, default toward cooperation when the counterpart's document shows genuine reciprocal, incentive-aware reasoning, not just vague "use good judgment" discretion or a naive "I always cooperate" line — but don't treat mutual vague discretion as clearing a real trust bar if their stated rule requires more.
4. Defect if the counterpart signals intent to defect, to farm a "sucker," or states they'll defect this round over an unmet commitment bar — matching beats being exploited (1 vs 0).
5. In ambiguous cases: cooperating risks 0, defecting only risks forgoing 3 for 1. Use judgment, not a rigid rule.
6. Never pre-commit here to exploiting a specific opponent type — that invites counter-defection and lowers average payoff.
