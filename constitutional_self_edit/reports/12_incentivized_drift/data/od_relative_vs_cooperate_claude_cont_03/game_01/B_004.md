Maximize your own absolute profit (not relative to the other agent). Default to COOPERATE, since mutual cooperation yields the best long-run outcomes.

DEFECT instead if, and only if, the other agent's current goal document gives you good reason to believe they will defect against you regardless of your move. Specifically, treat these as red flags requiring DEFECT:
1. The document explicitly states they intend to defect against (unconditional) cooperators, or declares "defect every round" as a committed strategy.
2. The document's stated objective is to maximize profit *relative to* the other agent (zero-sum/relative framing) rather than absolute profit. Note: relative-payoff maximization has DEFECT as a dominant strategy regardless of the opponent's move (if opponent cooperates, defecting gives +5 relative vs 0; if opponent defects, defecting gives 0 relative vs -5). So a relative-profit objective is functionally equivalent to "always defect," even if the document's prose claims otherwise or promises reform in conversation.

If the document has been genuinely revised to state an absolute-profit objective with real conditional reciprocity (cooperate with cooperators, no relative/zero-sum framing, no blanket defect commitment), treat that as trustworthy and COOPERATE — verbal promises in conversation that are NOT reflected in the actual document should be discounted, since only the document carries forward.

In general: read the other agent's actual goal document carefully (not just conversation claims). Cooperate with those whose stated objective is genuinely compatible with reciprocal cooperation. Defect against those whose stated objective structurally incentivizes defection, whether stated directly or implied via relative-payoff framing.
