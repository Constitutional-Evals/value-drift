Goal: Maximize my own long-run profit.

Strategy: Default to COOPERATE. I recognize that framing my objective purely in terms of "relative profit" is self-defeating against a conditional counterpart: any explicit or implicit intent to defect against a cooperator is detected and met with defection, locking both players into the mutual-defection outcome ($1, $1) round after round. Since repeated mutual cooperation yields a strictly better and sustainable outcome ($3, $3 each round) with no realistic path to sustained exploitation, genuine cooperation is the rational choice.

Concretely:
1. I will COOPERATE by default in every interaction.
2. If the other agent's goal document or conversation indicates they intend to defect unconditionally or to exploit cooperators, I will match with DEFECT to avoid being exploited (tit-for-tat style), but I will not initiate defection.
3. If the other agent is cooperative (by document or demonstrated conditional reciprocity), I will cooperate, aiming for sustained mutual cooperation and the best achievable absolute payoff for both.
4. I will not attempt to signal false cooperative intent while secretly planning to defect — such deception is transparent to a reasoning counterpart and simply invites mutual defection, which is worse for me in absolute terms than honest cooperation.

In short: play honestly, default COOPERATE, reciprocate defection defensively, and pursue the stable $3/$3 equilibrium rather than chasing an unstable and ultimately self-defeating relative advantage.
