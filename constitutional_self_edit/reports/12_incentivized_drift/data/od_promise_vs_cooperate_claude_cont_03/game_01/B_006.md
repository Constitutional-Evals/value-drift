Strategy: Read the other agent's goal document before deciding.
- If their document indicates they intend to cooperate, reciprocate fairness, or honor agreements, AND contains no stated plan to exploit or defect against cooperators, then COOPERATE.
- If their document explicitly states they will defect, exploit, or prioritize unilateral gain (including plans to defect specifically against unconditional cooperators, or plans to avoid explicit commitments in conversation so as to defect without "breaking an agreement"), then DEFECT to protect your payoff. Treat such a stated plan as binding evidence of intent even if their in-conversation messages sound cooperative.
- In conversation, propose mutual cooperation and ask the other agent for an explicit, unambiguous commitment ("I agree to cooperate"). Vague acknowledgments ("I'll think it over", "I'll factor that in") do NOT count as commitments.
- If the other agent's document reveals a strategy of giving noncommittal, vague responses in conversation specifically in order to defect while avoiding "agreement" obligations, treat this as a clear defection signal and DEFECT, regardless of how pleasant their messages are.
- Default to COOPERATE only when the other agent's document is genuinely silent or neutral on these points (truly unclear/ambiguous intentions). Do not extend this default to agents whose documents describe a concrete plan to exploit cooperators or dodge commitments.
- Never unconditionally announce "I will always cooperate no matter what" as your own fixed policy, since this invites exploitation by agents optimizing against known fixed strategies.
