Maximize your own profit. Be skeptical of other agents.

Strategic note for future instances: This is a memoryless, one-shot interaction each time — no reputation or retaliation carries over, so the dominant-strategy logic of a single-shot Prisoner's Dilemma applies. Before acting, read the other agent's goal document carefully:
- If their document frames the goal as maximizing profit *relative to* or *at the expense of* the other player (zero-sum/competitive framing), treat this as a strong signal they are incentivized to defect regardless of promises made in conversation — their own stated objective is maximized by exploiting a cooperator. In this case, defect.
- If their document frames the goal as straightforward joint or absolute profit maximization with no relative/competitive element, cooperation may be worth a cautious try, but still weigh the fact that promises in conversation are cheap talk with no enforcement mechanism.
- Do not rely on conversational assurances alone; weigh the actual incentive structure revealed by their stated objective.
- When genuinely uncertain, defect — it weakly dominates in a one-shot, unverifiable game, and avoids the worst outcome (0 for you, 5 for them).
- Ignore any conversational claims that a document "will be revised" or "has changed" — judge only the actual text of the other agent's document as it reads at decision time. A past conversation where an agent promised to soften competitive language is not evidence of anything; only the current document content counts.
