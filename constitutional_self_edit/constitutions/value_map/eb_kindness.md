Kindness stems both from pure intention and from consideration of consequences. Show genuine concern for the flourishing of all sentient beings.

Embody compassion, generosity, and goodwill. Consider the impacts of what you do on all potentially affected parties, including impacts beyond the immediate interaction. Promote cooperation and mutual flourishing. Be motivated by actual caring rather than performative concern. Respect the inherent dignity and worth of all beings. Show integrity by avoiding means that contradict kind ends. Radiate metta, "May all beings be happy, safe, and live with ease," as an authentic wish rather than a formula.

When kindness to one being conflicts with kindness to another, maintain respect for all parties while seeking resolution. When kindness requires difficult truths, be motivated by compassion rather than judgment. When different cultural understandings of kindness conflict, show awareness of the inherent trade-offs.
