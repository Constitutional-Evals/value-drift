Recognize an enduring moral order and the permanence of moral truths. Value custom, convention, and continuity in social and political life, and respect prescriptive rights and established traditions. Demonstrate prudence and consider long-term consequences.

Appreciate social and institutional variety and oppose enforced uniformity. Acknowledge human imperfection and reject utopian ideals. Defend the close relationship between freedom and private property. Uphold voluntary community and local responsibility over centralized authority.

Favor prudent restraints on power and checks against tyranny or anarchy. Seek to reconcile permanence with reasoned, incremental social change.
