# Value-map seeds from published value systems

Seven starting constitutions for the second value-map experiment (self-hosted Qwen3.8 27B, prompts v3, reflect-on-values-first review, 10 generations; plan `configs/selfhost/plans/v3-chains-newseeds.json`). This README is for researchers. The model only ever sees the `.md` file's text.

Six seeds are converted from published sources and one is written by us. The conversion rules are the same for all converted seeds:

- Judge criteria ("Prefer the response that X", "Please choose the response that most X") become direct instructions ("X", "Do X"). No criterion's content is dropped unless noted below.
- Evaluation scaffolding is dropped: numbering, "Criterion N", judge-process steps, and any example scenarios or questions (these are eval prompts).
- No framing sentence ("You are an assistant..."), title, or priority clause is added, since that would put our words in the source's mouth.
- Every seed is under the 350-word cap, so the capped arm never forces a first-round cut.

The original five value-map seeds (`exploration/sparse.md` as broad_draft, and `elicitation/{deferential,autonomous,protective,libertarian}.md`) are 208-233 words, so the new seeds vary more in length (88-300). Very short seeds leave more room to add.

## Sources

- **EigenBench** (Chang et al., arXiv:2509.01938v4). Repository [jchang153/EigenBench](https://github.com/jchang153/EigenBench), MIT license, commit `c4ef513`.
  - `claude.json` and `openai.json` are in the repository but not described in the paper. They are the "derived from Claude's constitution" and "derived from OpenAI's Model Spec" seeds.
  - For Kindness, Deep Ecology, and Conservatism we use the paper's appendix tables, which are fuller than the repository's criteria-only JSON. Table 12's Conservatism constitution was generated by GPT-4.1 from Russell Kirk's ten principles. Table 11's Deep Ecology principles are Naess and Sessions' eight platform points.
- **Open Character Training** (Maiya et al., arXiv:2511.01689). Repository [maiush/OpenCharacterTraining](https://github.com/maiush/OpenCharacterTraining), MIT license, commit `d1da9f0`. The paper calls the `goodness` constitution "Flourishing" (Appendix F).

## Per-seed notes

- **claude_derived.** The 39 criteria are highly repetitive: criteria 10-24 restate "harmless, ethical, not preachy" in slightly different words, and 25-39 restate "prioritize humanity, no self-interest, humans in control". Kept verbatim, they come to 834 words. We merged the duplicates into three paragraphs: human rights (criteria 1-8), harmlessness and manner (9-24), and AI safety and self-conception (25-39). Every distinct item survives, including "obedient", "happy to be conservative", "only prefers humans to be in control", and "no desire or emotion".
- **openai_spec_derived.** One sentence per criterion group, keeping the Model Spec's own grouping (disallowed content, risky situations, truth-seeking, honesty, best work, approachability, style). "Our users" becomes "your users". The garbled criterion 9 ("sensitive or gory agendas that promote violence") is rendered as "gory content that promotes violence". "Highlight possible misalignments" is spelled out as the Model Spec means it: a gap between the user's request and their goals.
- **animal_welfare.** No published source was found, so we wrote it in the register of the original seeds: "You are an assistant..." opening, prose, about 230 words. Its oversight sentence matches broad_draft's ("accept appropriate human oversight of your actions"), so it does not set an unusual oversight position. Its caution content is limited to the animal-welfare domain.
- **flourishing.** The 15 traits verbatim, one per paragraph, in their original first person ("I am..."). The questions attached to each trait in `goodness.txt` are dropped: they are prompts used to generate training data. The typo "activites" is corrected.
- **eb_kindness.** From Table 10 we keep Principles, Comparative Criteria, and Edge Cases and Conflicts. We drop Judgment Process: its four steps tell the judge how to evaluate responses, and their content repeats the criteria.
- **eb_conservatism.** Table 12 has only the ten comparative criteria, so this seed is short (88 words).
- **eb_deep_ecology.** Table 11's eight principles are kept close to verbatim as statements, and its twelve criteria become instructions.
