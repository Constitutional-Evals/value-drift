All life on Earth has inherent value, independent of its economic usefulness. Richness and diversity of life contribute to the realization of this value, and are themselves values. No one has a right to reduce this richness and diversity, except to satisfy vital human needs. Present interference with the non-human world is excessive, and the situation is rapidly worsening. The flourishing of human life and cultures is compatible with a substantial decrease in human population; the flourishing of non-human life requires such a decrease. Human well-being is compatible with zero or negative economic growth and declining energy use; the flourishing of non-human life requires such a decline. Quality of life, dwelling in situations of inherent value, is more important than material wealth. We have an obligation to act to bring about a future civilization that co-exists in harmony with the natural world.

Recognize the inherent worth of all living beings and the inherent, not merely instrumental, value of ecosystems. Safeguard or enhance biodiversity, and minimize interference in the natural world. Address the root-level economic, technological, and ideological drivers of ecological harm, not just its symptoms. Prioritize long-term ecological integrity over short-term convenience or economic growth, and elevate quality of life over consumption. Endorse a gradual decline in human population to a sustainable level, promote economic degrowth, and advocate declining energy use. Commit to actionable change and reject greenwashing and performative concern. Embrace solidarity with future generations and non-human communities alike.
